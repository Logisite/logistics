# Customer identity through verified contact points and shadow accounts

Customers hold accounts keyed to **verified contact points** rather than passwords. The canonical anchor is a phone number normalised to E.164: couriers operationally require a callable number anyway, numbers are quasi-permanent, and they give a deterministic join key. Email, WhatsApp, and Messenger attach as additional verified points on the same user UUID. A **shadow account** is the entry state: minted silently as a side effect of the customer's first confirmed action (accepting a pre-priced booking from a signed capability link), carrying a configurable TTL (`SHADOW_ACCOUNT_TTL_DAYS`). Expiry freezes self-service login only; it deletes nothing: bookings, history, and contact points persist until the operator explicitly builds deletion tooling. Verification always happens inside the channel where trust already exists (link tap in WhatsApp/Messenger, email magic link, code read aloud); SMS never appears. Device identity is a first-party client UUID used to re-link returning shadows and deduplicate accounts: an optimiser, never a credential. This amends ADR 0003: email remains mandatory for staff roles; for customers it is optional metadata.

We chose this over email-first registration, which adds five friction steps to a phone-first audience before any value is shown, and over SMS-OTP-only identity, which locks us to one channel and collides with the standing no-SMS architecture. Capability links (HMAC-signed payloads granting exactly one action) carry authorisation before an account exists; possession of the chat message proves control of the contact point. It is hard to reverse because every consumer surface (booking forms, tracking pages, notification fan-out, merge logic) assumes phone-anchored users with attachable channels, and because silent account creation must remain a side effect rather than become a gate. It is surprising because a customer can hold a full delivery history without ever having seen a signup form, and because expired accounts lose access while losing nothing.
